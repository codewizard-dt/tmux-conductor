## [2026-08-22] bug | monitor.sh false "usage limit hit" auto-teardown fixed
Root-caused a repeated tmux-session crash/flap discovered during live Playwright-driven UAT verification (session died 3 times in one afternoon): `scripts/monitor.sh`'s poll loop initializes `all_idle=true`/`all_usage_hit=true` at the top of each tick, then for any agent whose pane is missing (`tmux has-session` fails), it only logged a WARN and `continue`d — never falsifying either flag.
